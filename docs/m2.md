# Godspark M2: leveling to 100 and real-time d20 combat

## Context

M1 ("Dig together") is on main and the user has played it: movement feels right. The next
milestone was "Fight" (characters level 1-3, d20 combat). The user wants it reshaped into an
ARPG in an SRD setting: levels run to 100, every fifth level is a D&D class level, the
levels between need their own rewards, the player fights far more monsters than tabletop
D&D, gear matters more, and the 3-item attunement cap goes. It should be quirky and
stylized. The overall project plan stays in the repo at `docs/plan.md`; this replaces its M2.

Decisions made with the user (2026-11-05):

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Level map | Class level 1 at creation; levels 5, 10 ... 95 are class levels 2 to 20; level 100 is the first step past mortal (an Epic Boon, start of the godhood track) |
| Mid levels | A fixed rhythm of found sparks, Vigor and Knacks (below) |
| Monsters | SRD stat blocks as written; volume from packs and minions, challenge from elites, champions and bosses; danger by depth and plane; no level scaling |
| Resources | SRD spell slots and per-rest abilities; short rest at a campfire, long rest in a bed (the night passes for the world) |
| Gear limit | Body slots only, ten rings; no attunement cap |
| Loot | SRD magic items as named treasures; ordinary gear gets materials, quality and small affixes |
| Spark style | An inked constellation on the character that becomes their holy symbol |
| Death | Downed with death saves and revives; dying sends you to your bed or a shrine with nothing lost |

## The level track

`class_level = min(20, 1 + level / 5)`. Each band of five levels has the same beat, so
players learn it:

| Level in band | Reward |
|---|---|
| +1 | **Spark slot**: one more star in your constellation |
| +2 | **Vigor**: extra max hit points (half a hit die) and one more Hit Point Die for rests |
| +3 | **Spark slot** |
| +4 | **Knack**: pick one of three small permanent perks |
| +5 | **Class level**: everything the SRD gives (features, slots, proficiency, feats) |

- Every level also adds a fifth of the next class level's hit points, so the bar always
  grows and totals match the SRD at each class level (Vigor is on top).
- 40 spark slots, 20 Vigor steps and 20 Knacks by level 100.
- **Knacks** are offered from what you did in that band (the game counts deeds: tiles dug,
  distance fallen, trades with a people, kills by creature type): a skill or tool
  proficiency, a language, or a **Bond** with a people or a kind of beast (better prices,
  safe passage, animals that don't attack). This is where "relationships" live.
- XP: the SRD gap between class levels split into five, times one scale constant. Monster
  XP is the stat block's, times a rank multiplier. Everyone nearby gets full XP (no split).
  Target: early levels in 3-6 minutes, level 50 about 20, the 90s about an hour.

## Sparks

Sparks are scraps of divinity found in the world: on elites and champions, in ruins and
lairs, at standing stones. A slot is capacity; the world supplies what fills it. Loose
sparks are inventory items, so they can be traded. Kindle or swap them at a shrine.

- **The rule that keeps balance:** sparks never touch attack rolls, AC, save DCs or damage
  dice. Class and gear decide how you fight; sparks decide how you move and live.
- First set, each in ranks I-III (higher ranks deeper):
  - Motion: Fleet (run speed), Spring (jump), Skyhook (air jump), Dash, Featherfall, Mole
    (dig speed), Longarm (reach), Gecko (wall cling), Otter (swim).
  - Senses: Lantern (light radius), Owl (see further in dim light), Magnet (pickup
    radius), Dowser (ore glints nearby).
  - Tempo: Quickhand (swing recovery), Steady (cast while moving), Surefoot (no slowdown
    when hit).
  - Bonds and fortune: Silver Tongue (prices), Hearthfriend (reputation gain),
    Beastfriend, Scavenger (more drops).
- **Look:** each kindled spark is a star inked on the skin; lines join them in the order
  kindled and glow when a spark triggers. The drawing is saved as the character's sigil and
  is what worshippers carry in the godhood track.

## Combat feel

- **Tempo.** A swing's time comes from the weapon (light fast, heavy slow) and every swing
  is a real SRD attack roll through the addon's `AttackResolver`. Extra Attack and
  Multiattack become combos, so damage ratios between builds match the SRD while the pace
  is an ARPG's. Bonus action and reaction are their own 2 s timers. All numbers live in
  `data/combat.json`; this needs the same play-and-tune loop movement got.
- **Hits are physical.** Melee arcs, projectiles and spell areas must touch the target
  before anything is rolled; a dodged attack never rolls. Monster attacks have readable
  wind-ups (Wayfarer's convention, about half a second).
- **Durations** come from a table, not a formula: a round is 2 s, a minute 20 s, ten
  minutes 2 real minutes, an hour 5, a day one game day.
- **Rests.** Short rest: 5 s undisturbed at a campfire you place (costs wood, no enemies
  near). Long rest: sleep in a bed once a game day; when the players are in bed the night
  passes and the world sim catches up.
- **Downed.** Death saves on a slow timer; an ally's hand or a potion gets you up. On
  death you wake at your bed or the nearest shrine with nothing lost.

## Monsters

- **Ranks.** Minion (dies to any hit that lands, quarter XP), Standard (the stat block),
  Elite (maximum hit points, one affix, a chance of a spark), Champion (double that, two
  affixes, a guaranteed spark or item), Boss (lair creatures with their legendary actions).
- **Affixes** act through real-time and tile mechanics, not bigger numbers: Frenzied
  (faster tempo), Shielded (a ward to break first), Splitting, Burrowing, Lantern-eater
  (dims nearby light), Tethered (shares damage with a packmate), Gilded (sheds coin).
- **Where they are.** `data/ecology/habitats.json` maps each SRD monster to biome, depth
  band, time of day, pack shape and behaviour. A spawn director on the host fills each
  player's surroundings off-screen from a CR budget set by depth and plane.
- **Lairs are real.** A sim lair's creatures ("4 grick") are spawned as those creatures
  when a player arrives, and kills are written back to the sim. This is the first piece of
  materialization.
- **Brains.** `HostEntity.brain` already exists for this. Archetypes follow Wayfarer's:
  bruiser, skirmisher, heavy, support, plus flyer, lurker and swarm, chosen from the stat
  block with overrides in data. They need tile navigation with jumps and drops, which does
  not exist yet.
- **Peoples.** The addon gains `make_npc(role, species, lineage)` so bandits, guards and
  drow raiders are SRD NPC stat blocks with species traits.

## Gear

- **Slots:** head, neck, cloak, armour, hands, belt, feet, main hand, off hand, ten rings.
  If it fits, it works. Items the SRD marks as needing attunement bond after a moment at a
  campfire, with no limit on how many.
- **Ordinary gear:** material from what is mined (copper, iron, silver, then the SRD's
  mithral and adamantine), quality from the smith (better in bigger settlements, which the
  sim already tracks), and up to three small affixes on drops. Total bonus to hit and AC
  stays within the SRD's +3; affixes add riders and utility instead.
- **Inventory and hotbar** arrive here too, which ends unlimited block placing: what you
  dig is what you can place.

## What gets reused

- Addon (`addons/srd`): `SRDContent.make_character/make_monster`, `SRDProgression`
  (choices and builds for class levels 1-20), `ClassKit`, `AttackResolver`,
  `SpellResolver`, `ActiveEffects`, `MonsterActions`, `SRDRest`, `SRDItems`,
  `ItemEffects`, `EquipmentSlots`, `Dice.rng`. The 100-level track is a layer in the game,
  not a change to the addon's 20 levels.
- Game: `HostEntity` (monster kind and `brain`), `WorldHost`, interest-filtered snapshots,
  `TileCollision.raycast` (line of sight and cover), `LightEngine.category_at` (SRD light
  for Stealth and Darkvision), `MoveStats.from_srd`, `Looks` and `ActorBody` (monster and
  people bodies, attack/hit/death clips), `WorldSim` lairs, the dressing-object records
  (campfires, beds and shrines are placed objects of the same kind).
- Wayfarer (`wayfarer/scripts`, `enemy_controller.gd`, `melee_attacker.gd`,
  `telegraph.gd`): patterns for real-time brains, contact-frame timing and telegraphs.

## How it gets built

I write the contracts first in `docs/architecture.md` (character record, item record,
combat event, the tables above as data schemas), then Opus agents build in worktrees, at
most three at a time, each reporting in `docs/tracks/`.

- **Wave 1**
  - Characters and leveling: `core/rules/` (level track, XP, Vigor, Knacks and deeds,
    spark slots, builds saved per player, rests, downed and death), host and net sync.
  - Combat: `core/combat/` (tempo, hit shapes, the bridge to the addon's resolvers,
    conditions and durations, combat events on the wire).
  - Items and sparks: inventory, slots, materials and affixes, loot tables, sparks and the
    constellation data, campfire, bed and shrine objects.
- **Wave 2**
  - Monsters: brains, tile navigation, the ecology table, spawn director, ranks and
    affixes, lair spawning with write-back, `make_npc` in the addon.
  - Interface: HUD, character sheet, level-up and Knack screens, inventory with the
    ten-ring paper doll, the constellation drawing, damage numbers, telegraphs, attack
    animation hooks.
- **Then** an integration pass, and you play it.

First cut of content: Fighter, Rogue, Wizard and Cleric, checked through class level 5
(level 24: Extra Attack and Fireball), surface and cave monsters, about twenty sparks. The
systems are built for all 100 levels; later classes and deeper bands are content.

## Verification

- `godot --headless -s res://tools/run_tests.gd` prints `ALL PASS`: the level map and hit
  point totals against the SRD at every class level, reward rhythm, spark rule (no spark
  changes an attack roll, AC, DC or damage die), slot rules, rest and death rules.
- `tools/arena.gd` (headless): scripted fights of a build against a pack; prints time to
  kill and damage rates, and checks build-to-build ratios against the SRD's.
- `tools/xp_pacing.gd`: expected kills and minutes per level across the 100, to set the
  XP scale against the pacing target.
- `tools/net_smoke.sh` extended: two bots fight a spawned pack on a host; hit points,
  loot and levels match on every side.
- Screenshots through `tools/soft_godot.sh` of a fight, the constellation and the
  paper doll, which I look at.
- You play it. Tempo, wind-ups and how hordes feel can only be judged by hand.

## Carried rules

- Rules content is SRD 5.2.1; sparks, affixes, ranks and Knacks are original. No Forgotten
  Realms names. Never commit art. Nothing is pushed without your OK.
- `docs/plan.md` in the repo gets this M2 and the changed decisions (100 levels, no
