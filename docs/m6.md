# Godspark M6: Underdark peoples

The milestone after [m5.md](m5.md) (biomes and the overworld) and the lighting track
([lighting.md](lighting.md)). `docs/plan.md` once called this "The Deep". The user's founding
line for it: a massive Underdark, where "underdark denizens like drow might raid trade caravans".

## Decisions (my defaults, 2026-11-07, for the user to overturn)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Shape | The descent is continuous: caves, deeps, nadir are one connected underground with no gates, reached from the surface by cave mouths, under-routes, buried ruins and sinkholes. The Hells come later, below the nadir |
| Underground biomes | The four already in the vocabulary become real regions: `fungal` (deeps, wet, food), `crystal` (deeps, dry, ore), `deep_lake` (a sea in the deeps with shores and islands), `magma` (nadir). Plain `underdark` rock between them |
| Peoples | Drow city-states (elf, drow lineage: raiders, scouts, mages, priests), deep dwarves as dwarf (deep) holds (the word "duergar" is SRD 5.1, not 5.2.1: renamed in integration), deep gnome (svirfneblin) warrens as gnome, goblinoid and kobold warrens as they are, all SRD NPC stat blocks through `make_npc` |
| What they do | The same sim as the surface: food, goods, trade along deep roads, wars, and **raids upward**: a drow city plans raids on the surface roads it can reach, with the barrier routes (richest) first |
| Danger | Depth sets CR as now (caves 0.5-5, deeps to 10, nadir to 15); lair bosses in the deeps are the first real bosses: aboleth in the deep lake, purple worm and bulette as burrowers that dig, roper and cloaker in the dark |
| Light | The lighting track makes the dark matter; the Underdark is built on it: fungal glow, crystal light, magma light are emissive tiles, drow cities are lamp-lit, the rest is black |
| Levels | Content to class level 10 (level 45) across caves and deeps; the nadir holds levels beyond |

Hazards (poison air, heat) still out. Mind flayers do not exist in the SRD and are not faked.

In the worlds now:

- Regions in the deeps and nadir, the sealed deep lake with its lair on an island, lava, sinkholes,
  all four kinds of way in; surface to nadir and back on foot on medium seeds 1-5, `digs=0`.
- Drow, deep dwarves (the SRD 5.2.1 word is plain dwarf: "duergar" is 5.1), deep gnomes, goblin and
  kobold warrens in lamp-lit towns; deep roads carrying goods; humans, halflings and tieflings go
  down the way at their own town; the toll, and standing with the drow.
- Raids from below by the ramp and the sinkholes, guards at the mouth, `below` bounties; lairs by
  ground with the aboleth in the lake on most worlds; burrowers that dig.

## What this milestone delivers

1. **Underground biomes in worldgen.** Regions within the deeps and nadir: fungal forests
   (glowing caps as dressing and light), crystal caverns (emissive crystal tiles, ore), the deep
   lake (a real body of water with swimming, which exists in `movement.json` but no world uses
   yet; shores, islands, boats later), magma (lava tiles that burn, light). Each region names
   itself on the map once seen. Built: [tracks/deep.md](tracks/deep.md).
2. **The descent is a journey.** From any surface biome a player can reach the nadir on foot,
   through at least three kinds of way (cave mouth, under-route, sinkhole or buried ruin), with
   the climb back possible. `tools/crossing.gd` gains a `descent` mode: surface to nadir and
   back, `digs=0`. Built: [tracks/deep.md](tracks/deep.md); seed 3's walk back fixed in integration.
3. **Peoples below.** Drow cities, deep dwarf holds, deep gnome warrens, goblin and kobold warrens
   placed by the sim's culture weights on real underground biomes, built from kits, lamp-lit,
   with their people, guards, shops, smiths, quests and patrons as surface towns have. Deep roads
   are trade routes with underdark goods (ore, crystal, fungal reagents, salt from magma).
4. **Raids from below.** A drow city (and goblinoid warrens) plans raids on surface roads it can
   reach by a known way up, preferring the valuable barrier roads; the raid walks up, ambushes,
   and carries the goods home, where the town's books receive them. Surface towns answer with
   guards at cave mouths and bounties (quests) that lead the player down. Standing with the
   drow is a faction like any other: a player can trade with them.
5. **Lair bosses and burrowers.** Aboleth in the deep lake (a lair with legendary actions, and
   its charmed servants), purple worm and bulette that really dig (Burrow as a movement mode
   that removes tiles and leaves rubble), roper and cloaker ambushes in the dark, grick and
   darkmantle packs, oozes in the fungal wet. Lairs are sim records that materialize, as now.
6. **The look below.** Dressing per underground biome, the lighting track's emissive sources
   per biome, fog of the deep, sound later. Drow architecture from the kit.

## How it gets built

Reporting in `docs/tracks/`:

1. **Deep biomes and the descent**: worldgen regions, lake, magma, emissive tiles, descent
   routes, the tool and test. Contract: `meta.biomes` gains underground regions with bounds.
2. **Peoples below**: sim placement on real deep biomes, kits, deep roads as trade, goods,
   `make_npc` roles for drow, deep dwarf, deep gnome; Life materializes them like surface towns.
3. **Raids from below and the deep life**: raid planning upward, ambush and return, bounties,
   lairs with bosses, burrowers, the deep lake's aboleth.
4. **Integration**: descent end to end, a drow raid on a caravan seen by two players and answered
   by a bounty taken down to the city, docs. Built: [tracks/m6-integration.md](tracks/m6-integration.md).
5. **The look below**: deliverable 6. Then the user plays.
